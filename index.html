Tell us how you use AI

AI consumes real natural resources. This is an informal census to understand how people are using AI, and whether that usage is worth the energy it costs.

What we believe
✓

Coding to build something useful -- using AI to help bring an idea to life that serves others is a fair use of the resource.

✓

Research and science -- accelerating discovery, analysing data, exploring problems that benefit humanity.

✓

Students learning -- AI as a tutor, not a shortcut. Understanding what it gives you, not just copying it.

✓

Creative exploration -- AI art for personal creativity and experimentation, not for selling as your own work.

!

Business use needs scrutiny -- write your business plan yourself. AI should augment your thinking, not replace it.

✕

AI-generated social media content -- flooding feeds with synthetic content wastes energy and erodes trust.

✕

Generating harmful imagery -- this is never acceptable. Full stop.

✕

Fake news and narrative-pushing -- AI-generated journalism or media created to push an agenda is a misuse of the resource.

✕

Selling AI output as human-made -- if AI made it, say so. Don't profit from a lie.
